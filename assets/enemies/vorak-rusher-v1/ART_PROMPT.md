## Prompt exact

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: single transparent top-down 2D spaceship sprite for the ordinary early-game enemy "Vorak rusher", an agile small interceptor in a space MMO.
Primary request: redesign this common enemy from scratch as a compact, aggressive red and dark gunmetal fighter. It is a modest regular enemy, NOT a boss. A clear crafted silhouette that reads at 65-75 pixels in game.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background, no scene, no floor, no shadow underneath, no frame, no label.
Subject and structure: a short central faceted armored fuselage with a narrow pointed nose at exactly 12 o'clock; two short swept triangular side wings/claws, one per side, connected by small mechanical hinges. The wings extend sideways and rearward, not in a ring. Two small physical engine nozzles at the rear left and rear right at 6 o'clock. A small narrow red energy slit at the front is the weapon; no giant circular glowing orb. Three main simple masses: central body and two short flanking wing assemblies. Compact wide wedge / dart proportions, slightly longer than wide (about 1.2 to 1). Rear engines clearly visible as two small warm bright slots. Thin transparent gaps define side wings from central body where possible, leaving hinges readable for later articulation.
Style/medium: premium pre-rendered 2D game sprite, orthographic view directly from above, sculpted crisp metal forms and clean readable paneling. Restrained microdetail; readable major planes, no noisy thin spikes.
Composition/framing: ONE complete ship centered on a square image, nose straight up, bilateral symmetry, fills about 78% of image height and 66% of width with transparent padding on all sides.
Lighting/mood: subtle neutral top lighting makes charcoal metal readable on deep space, modest crimson energy accents; the engine slots have pale hot centers but NO baked flame or smoke. The red plates remain rich red, not all glowing.
Color palette: dark graphite and gunmetal structural center, deep crimson/red armor on short outer wings and nose, a few silver bevels, tiny warm red-orange lit seams. Avoid blue, purple, large orange panels.
Constraints: common small interceptor proportions, no huge limbs, no crown, no decorative aura, no rings, no boss scale, no giant core, no tentacles, no rotating circular assembly, no long needle tail. No plume, exhaust trail, muzzle flash or particles baked into the image: these will be animated separately. No text, watermark, checkerboard or black opaque background.
```
