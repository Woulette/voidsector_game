## Prompt final

Create ONE new game-ready transparent enemy sprite for the FIRST, COMMON LOW LEVEL MONSTER of a strict orthographic 2D TOP-DOWN space shooter. Reference image 1 is the approved BOSS: use its cold blue, silver and navy material style and family identity, but make this new enemy much simpler, compact, modest and friendly to read as the game's weakest regular enemy. Reference image 2 is the old common orb: retain its small round body and short little radial legs. The new common orb must have exactly SIX SHORT LITTLE radial metallic legs, evenly spaced at 60 degree intervals, no long limbs, no elaborate wings, no boss crown. A modest clear blue reactor nucleus is centered in one simple dark blue-grey circular metal housing. Housing radius is approximately 26 percent of total sprite diameter; a single simple segmented pewter rim and subtle panel seams, no nested ornate jewelry, fewer luminous nodes than the boss. Six short narrow tapered legs occupy radii 30 to 43 percent of total sprite diameter, at angles 0,60,120,180,240,300 degrees. Each leg has one small dark joint, one simple tapered silver plate and one small cyan inset near its root. Keep all six separate from the round housing through a thin genuinely transparent annular gap at radius 27 to 29 percent, because the six legs will rotate around a stationary core in the game. Core and housing remain a perfect circle. The legs must be small and restrained, NOT six long elaborate boss arms. The entire silhouette should feel like a compact basic scout drone, clearly a simpler juvenile member of the same family as reference 1, still polished professional game art. Deep navy, blue-grey metal, restrained cold silver edge highlights and cyan blue nucleus, lower contrast and less dominant glow than the boss. Perfect directly overhead camera at 90 degrees, no isometric perspective, centered radial symmetry, balanced shape. Crisp readable materials designed to stay legible as a 48 pixel game sprite. Transparent background with real alpha, no smoky cloud, no stars, no scene, no text, no labels, no UI, no ground or cast floor shadow, no huge aura. Single assembled sprite only, centered, all six tips safely inside the image with at least 7 percent transparent margin. Do not copy the eight long branch boss silhouette. This is a NEW SMALL COMMON ORB inspired by the two references, not an edit to the boss.
