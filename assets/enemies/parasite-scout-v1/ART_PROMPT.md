# Parasite commun — première base animée

Illustration générée avec l’outil intégré `image_gen`, le 25 octobre 2026. L’ancien Parasite sert de référence pour l’identité verte et organique ; la nouvelle illustration est conservée séparément, sans remplacer le sprite public. PNG transparent original : `parasite-source.png`.

Six tentacules souples, carapace sombre, noyau vert et veines lumineuses. Les déformations, le flash au tir et la brume sont ajoutés par le renderer local. La variante boss sera préparée après examen de cette base commune.

Asset type: one transparent 2D top-down sprite, a small common "Parasite astral" enemy for the existing space MMO.

Body: dark olive and charcoal-green chitinous shell with a few smooth protective plates, organic pores and small glowing acid sacs. Central oval translucent green nucleus at the EXACT image center (50%,50%), radius about 17% of the square image. The nucleus has layered translucent membranes, a luminous lime interior and a small warm yellow-white focus. A few branching luminous green veins connect the nucleus to the six tentacle roots. The dark ridges and brighter membranes should read clearly on deep space.
Style: high quality pre-rendered 2D game creature sprite, true orthographic directly from above, premium sculpted biological forms with restrained detail and clean silhouette, compatible in finish with the approved VoidSector Orbs and Vorak sprites. Compact common-enemy design, not a boss. The six tentacles are the characteristic feature and should have visible green stripes/veins and a few tiny acid nodules, not endless thin hairs.
Composition: ONE complete centered organism on a square image, complete uncropped tips. Main body occupies central 35%; limbs reach about 42% from center, overall fills 82-85% of image. Modest common enemy, readable at about 70-75 pixels in game. No dramatic giant crown or oversized claws.
Palette: dark forest green and near-black organic shell, toxic lime-green membranes, small pale green highlights and a tiny yellow-white core focus. Keep the body distinct from the blue Orb and red Vorak. Lighting is neutral from above with local bioluminescent veins; do not bake an external glow halo.
Constraints: genuinely transparent alpha background. No scene, floor, cast shadow, frame, text, logo, watermark. No rings around the creature, no metallic spaceship parts, no big decorative energy aura. No smoke, spores, dust trail, attack flash or projectiles baked in; all those are animated separately. Do not make it look like a giant boss or a symmetrical mechanical star.
