Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone transparent sprite concept for an original orbital headquarters in Avosoma, a 2D top-down space MMO.
Primary request: a polished, believable compact command outpost with the sophistication and readable service-station silhouettes of classic space MMOs, using an original design.
Subject: a compact broad C-shaped armored command spine enclosing a small open-space docking gap; a solid faceted command roof behind the gap; two short forked docking arms opening toward image bottom; a few attached habitat / maintenance pods; slab radiators, exposed structural trusses and tidy external piping. The center must feel like a command facility and a sheltered docking gap, never a glowing portal or a huge flat landing platform. Strong readable silhouette with real empty space between modules.
Style/medium: high-quality hard-surface 3D render baked into a game sprite, grounded industrial spacecraft engineering, crisp at game size, moderate fine detail with broad readable material blocks.
Composition/framing: square image, entire object centered with 12 percent transparent padding. STRICT ORTHOGRAPHIC TOP DOWN, camera exactly 90 degrees overhead; see roofs and plan geometry, no isometric perspective, no foreshortened ellipse, no front elevation.
Lighting/mood: shared upper-left warm-neutral key light, restrained soft ambient occlusion, a very subtle copper rim; all solid hull surfaces opaque and well exposed, dark crevices remain detailed. Small warm-white utility lights and a few tiny cool instrument lights, no excessive glow.
Color palette/materials: satin gunmetal and dark steel structure, readable pale titanium roof panels, a few muted crimson armor plates and discreet red corporate light strips for Helion. Material roughness and careful panel joins; working maintained station with light use, not a wreck.
Constraints: truly transparent RGBA background, soft alpha only on anti-aliased silhouette and very small light bloom; NO black background, no stars, no nebula, no planet, no ships, no labels or lettering, no watermarks, no logo, no floor or terrain, no circular neon reactor, no giant weapons, no smoke. One isolated headquarters only. Aim for a 2048 by 2048 source sprite.
