# Texture des réacteurs, proposition du 24 octobre 2026

Génération : outil intégré `image_gen`, puis une retouche du halo par le même outil. Sortie RGBA avec transparence réelle. La première proposition géométrique a été retirée à la demande du créateur.

- Master retenu : `masters/thruster-plasma-v2.png`.
- Texture chargée par l'atelier : `thruster-plasma-v2.webp` (256 × 256 px, 33 932 octets), huit cellules de 64 × 128 px, alignées en quatre colonnes et deux rangées ; origine (32, 4). La conversion aligne l'origine lumineuse de chaque image et redimensionne les cellules ; elle ne détoure ni ne repeint le master.
- Assemblage : `../prepare_thruster_atlas.py`.
- Teinte et interpolation entre deux images : `../engines.js`. Les positions des moteurs et les palettes viennent du catalogue courant.

## Consigne de génération

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game VFX flipbook texture atlas for a realistic 2D top-down spaceship thruster, NOT an illustration or presentation. Create ONE seamless 8-frame animated exhaust sprite sheet on a genuinely transparent background. Exact layout: FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows, all eight cells perfectly aligned, no grid lines, no labels, no text. All cells depict the SAME small ion/plasma exhaust at consecutive closely spaced moments in a subtle 0.4-second loop. In every cell the jet starts at the SAME location: centered horizontally, at 16 percent of the cell height, pointing straight DOWN toward 88 percent of the cell height. Identical root position and overall scale in every frame. The source is a narrow flat round nozzle aperture only implied by the light; DO NOT draw any nozzle, metal, spaceship, hardware or surroundings. Premium semi-realistic space game VFX, detailed volumetric plasma, physically coherent flowing fine turbulence, pale icy cyan edges and a small white-hot cyan root, luminous internal filaments. Confined tight jet, a short dense core which softly tapers and dissipates, subtle asymmetric ragged edges, authentic translucent energy texture. Width no more than 22 percent of each cell, with ample empty transparent padding on every side. Extremely restrained soft bloom only immediately around the exhaust, no broad fog. No cartoon shapes, no oval blobs, no cone outline, no geometric shapes, no circles, no shock diamonds, no rings, no flying particles or sparks, no smoke cloud, no laser line, no lens flares, no shadow, no background, no frame borders. Orthographic image-plane view of the jet, no perspective. The result must be a clean usable RGBA sprite atlas ready to composite under a ship. Make it beautiful at small in-game scale, coherent across frames.

## Retouche ciblée

Edit this exact 4-column by 2-row, eight-frame thruster texture atlas. Change ONLY the broad surrounding haze/bloom and background alpha. Preserve the layout, all eight flame silhouettes, their positions and scale, all white/cyan internal fine plasma texture and their downward orientation. Remove the large blue and gray fog glow around each flame almost completely. Every pixel outside a very tight 3-to-5-pixel soft fringe around the detailed flame itself must be genuinely fully transparent. The clear regions between the eight jets and around every cell must have alpha zero, not dark blue or gray. Retain subtle translucent antialiasing on the ragged edges and fine filaments. This is a clean low-bloom production RGBA VFX atlas to composite onto small spaceships; the exhaust should be precisely contained, with rich material detail and no broad light cloud. No extra shapes, no hardware, no labels, no text, no background.
